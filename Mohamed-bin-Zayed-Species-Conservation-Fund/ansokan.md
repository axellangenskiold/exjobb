## Rules that matter for us

- **Form, not a document:** an online form in English, with fixed fields and word limits. Budget in US dollars.
- **Species:** priority for Endangered and Critically Endangered. African savanna elephant is Endangered (IUCN 2021), so we qualify. The status must be cited.
- **Amount:** two sizes, up to USD 5,000, or 5,000-25,000. Small local projects are preferred. We ask for **USD 5,000**.
- **Not funded:** core running costs and overheads, applications that cover only salary or travel, study courses, and mostly lab-based work. Living costs and stipends are allowed as part of a field project.
- **One application per project and species.** Two near-identical applications from Gustaf and Axel would compete with each other. **Send one application**, with Gustaf as applicant and Axel as key personnel (4.2). Axel doesn't make his own for this one.
- **CV upload is mandatory** (6.1), plus a project location map pin and one JPEG photo of the species, preferably taken in the wild.
- **1-3 references.** Not from your own organisation, and they must have an email on their organisation's own domain.
- **Permits:** the grantee is responsible for all permits.
- If funded: a report on the website every 6 months, and material for the Fund's website.
- About 12% of applications were funded (2019 figure).
